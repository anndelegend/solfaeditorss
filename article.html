How Tonic Solfa Works

A practical guide for singers.

Start with the scale.

Tonic solfa represents notes by their relationship to the key. The familiar syllables are do, re, mi, fa, sol, la and ti. This makes it especially useful for choir training, sight singing and understanding melodic movement.

d : r | m : f | s : l | t : d'

Learn by singing.

Solfa becomes useful when the symbols are connected to sound. Practise slowly, listen to intervals and learn to recognise how each degree feels in relation to the tonic.
